**BEng final-year research project · Tianjin University of Technology · June 2020–June 2022**

Supervised by Professor [Mian Zhang](https://me.tjut.edu.cn/info/1060/1786.htm).

I developed a dynamic sideband energy ratio method for diagnosing planetary gearbox faults under varying operating conditions. The work combined vibration signal processing with machine learning to identify useful fault-related features.

I used MATLAB for Fourier and wavelet analysis and investigated deep neural networks and support vector machines for parameter selection. The project received the Best Undergraduate Thesis Award, with the highest thesis grade of 93%.

**Related publication:** [Intelligent fault diagnosis of a planetary gearbox based on dynamic frequency energy ratio scheme](/publications/dynamic-frequency-energy-ratio/).
