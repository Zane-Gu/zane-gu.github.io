**MSc team research project · Imperial College London · October 2022–March 2023**

I worked on a motion-based handwriting recognition pen combining an inertial measurement unit (IMU), an embedded button, and a 3D-printed prototype. The button supported controlled data collection for lowercase character recognition.

The project investigated LSTM, bidirectional LSTM, GRU, CNN, and stacked ensemble models for learning from recorded motion signals. The team project received a mark of 91.7%.

**Related publication:** [Motion Is All You Need](/publications/motion-is-all-you-need/), ICMSP 2024.
