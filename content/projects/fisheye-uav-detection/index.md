**MSc individual research project · Imperial College London · December 2022–September 2023**

Supervised by Professor [Krystian Mikolajczyk](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/k.mikolajczyk) at [MatchLab](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/matchlab/).

I constructed fisheye datasets and investigated segmentation-based training for YOLO-based aerial object detection. The project explored using the Segment Anything Model (SAM) in a teacher–student strategy to improve the training of a real-time detector for UAV obstacle avoidance.

The resulting AirEyeSeg work examined detection robustness and generalization in fisheye imagery. My MSc project received a mark of 79.55%.

**Related publication:** [AirEyeSeg: Teacher-Student Insights into Robust Fisheye UAV Detection](/publications/aireyeseg/), recipient of the [Best Student Paper Award at ICPRAM 2024](https://icpram.scitevents.org/PreviousAwards.aspx#2024).
